Support

Need help with OMGains? Check the common questions below, or email us and we will get back to you.

Contact

For any question, bug report, or feature request, email support@omgains.app. Include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem, as it helps us reproduce it faster.

Where is my data stored?

All of your workouts, meals, and progress are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and no account is required. See the Privacy policy for details.

How do I back up or move my data?

In the app, open Settings and use the export options to create a backup file, photos included. You can save it anywhere you like and restore it from Settings on a new phone. Because your data lives only on your device, keeping a recent backup is the best way to protect it.

Reminders are not firing

Reminders use local notifications, so make sure notifications are allowed for OMGains in your device settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them.

I deleted my data by accident

Data removed with the Clear all data feature cannot be recovered by us, since we never have a copy. If you have a backup file, restore it from Settings.
