Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

OMGains is built to keep your personal information on your device. Your workouts, meals, history, and photos are stored locally and are never uploaded to us. A few specific features contact third-party services. This policy explains exactly what leaves your device and when.

What we store on your device

Workouts, exercises, meal recipes, grocery baskets, progress history, profile photo, theme, units, and other preferences are saved locally on your device using the operating system’s standard storage. We do not run servers that hold this data.

Ingredient search

When you search for an ingredient while building a recipe, the text you type is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a third-party open food database, to return suggestions and ingredient images. Only the search term is sent — not your recipes, meals, or any account information. If you never use ingredient search, no such requests are made.

App updates

The app may check for over-the-air updates so you receive fixes and improvements. This involves a request to the update service and does not transmit your personal data.

Permissions

The app requests permissions only when you tap a feature that needs them, and uses them solely for that purpose: your photo library and camera (and microphone, for video) to set a profile picture and add progress and recipe photos or videos; and notifications, so it can send the workout reminders you enable. You can decline or revoke any of these in your device settings.

Backups and exports

If you export or back up your data, the file is generated on your device and shared only through the destination you choose. OMGains does not send exported data anywhere on its own.

Deleting your data

You can clear all locally stored data at any time from More → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

Children

OMGains is a general-purpose fitness tracking app and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@omgains.app, or to the developer through the app’s store listing.
